# 7 signs a house will say YES (Reel script)

Target: 40-45 s, Instagram Reel. Film each line as its own clip (9 clips), 2 takes each.

| Clip | Line |
|---|---|
| HOOK | "Seven signs a house will say yes before you even knock. And most reps walk right past number six." |
| 1 | "One. Fresh landscaping. They're already spending money on the outside." |
| 2 | "Two. Kids' bikes in the driveway. Families are home after five, and they care about the house." |
| 3 | "Three. Two newer cars. There's room in the budget." |
| 4 | "Four. Another company's sign on the lawn. They already pay for home services." |
| 5 | "Five. A doorbell camera. That's usually an owner, not a renter." |
| 6 | "Six. The ugliest house on the street. Old roof, faded fence, cracked driveway. That's not a no. That's a need." |
| 7 | "Seven. Garage door open. They're home, and they're already in project mode." |
| CLOSE | "Hit these first, then work the rest of the street. Which one did I miss? Follow for more." |

Filming notes
- Vertical, chest-up, same spot and light for every clip. Start talking the moment you hit record; I cut the dead air.
- Say the number first on every line so the cut lands clean.
- Hook: do 3 versions with different energy; I pick the best.
- Optional B-roll: if you're out on a street, grab 3 s of any real example (landscaping, bikes, a contractor sign, an open garage). Real beats AI photos; anything missing uses the carousel photos.

Edit plan
- Hook on your face with the grey serif header (7 Signs / Say YES highlighted, "(most reps walk right past #6)").
- Each sign: the matching photo pops up as a card under your chin with the title, one-word captions, you in the corner on the photo beats.
- #6 gets an extra beat (zoom on the worn-out house) since the hook promises it.
- Close on your face, "Follow for more" text.
