# Can you sell $10,000 in 8 hours knocking doors? (Reel script)

Goal: broad reach. Real D2D numbers. Question -> answer -> new question. SalesMapper only in the last ~10 s ("we built").
Target ~40 s. Film each block as its own clip, 2 takes; hook 3 takes.

HOOK: "Can you sell ten thousand dollars in eight hours knocking doors? Let's do the math."
ANSWER 1: "Say you're selling a five-hundred-dollar service. That's twenty sales."
QUESTION 2: "Most reps close about two out of every hundred doors. So how many doors is that?"
ANSWER 2: "A thousand. In eight hours. That's a door every twenty-nine seconds."
TWIST: "Which is impossible. So the reps who actually have ten-K days aren't knocking more doors."
ANSWER 3: "They're selling bigger. At two grand a deal, it's five sales. Two hundred and fifty doors."
OBSTACLE: "That's still a door every two minutes. And here's what quietly kills it."
REVEAL: "Spend fifteen seconds logging every knock, and that's an hour of your day, gone."
PRODUCT: "That's why we built SalesMapper. You mark every door from your lock screen, and you're already walking to the next one."
CLOSE: "More time knocking. Less time tracking. Follow for more."

Math check: $10,000 / $500 = 20 sales; 20 / 2% = 1,000 doors; 8 h = 28,800 s / 1,000 = 28.8 s per door.
$10,000 / $2,000 = 5 sales; 5 / 2% = 250 doors; 28,800 / 250 = 115 s (about 2 min). 250 x 15 s = 3,750 s = 62.5 min.
Assumptions to confirm with real numbers: ~2% door-to-sale, 15 s to log a knock without SalesMapper, a $2,000 ticket existing in his niche.

Visual beats
- Hook: on face, TikTok header "$10,000 in 8 hours?" (bracket: "(let's do the math)").
- 20 sales: "$10,000 / $500 = 20 sales" equation pops in.
- 1,000 doors: house icons multiply fast; "1 door every 29 sec" timer.
- "Impossible": hard cut, push-in on face, red X over the 1,000.
- $2,000 deal: equation swaps to "5 sales / 250 doors".
- 15 s logging: real screen recording of unlocking a phone and logging in a map/notes app, a timer ticking 0:15; then "250 x 15 s = 1 hour" with a clock spinning.
- Product: the real lock-screen demo (cap_lock3.js capture), Sale tap -> pin drop.
- Close: face, red "Follow for more".
