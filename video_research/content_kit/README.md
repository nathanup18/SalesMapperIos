# Template post (v1)

template_post_preview.mp4 is a silent 30.5 s preview built to the short-form-video-edit skill (v2). Record the voiceover from script.txt and drop it on top.

Structure
- 0.0-2.6 s  B-roll, person walking (motion at frame 0, no face). Hook text at top from frame 0.
- 2.6-5.0 s  Suburb aerial. First cut at 2.6 s. Hook text off at 5.0 s.
- 5.0-12.0 s App: tap a house, pick "Sale", pin turns green.
- 12.0-20.5 s App: map fills with pins, zooms into a cluster of sales.
- 20.5-27.0 s App: Sales Summary, numbers count up.
- 27.0-30.5 s End card: icon, "Every door, one map.", "Free trial · link in bio".

Specs
- 1080x1920, 30 fps. Hook text: 6 words, white rounded box, Nunito Sans Black 68 px, top at y=250 px.
- Captions: word-by-word, 2 words per chunk, uppercase Nunito Sans Black 104 px, white with dark outline, active word green #34C759, centered at y=1344 (70% down).
- Voiceover: about 2.9 words/s, keep 0.4-0.6 s pauses between lines (top performers left longer pauses).

To make a new post
1. Swap the hook text in hook_overlay.png (or ask Claude to regenerate it).
2. Replace B-roll with your own walk-up and knock footage when you have it (better than stock).
3. Keep it 40-50 s for D2D by adding a talking segment; this template is the short version.
4. Re-time captions.ass to the recorded voiceover (Claude can do this from the audio with Whisper).
